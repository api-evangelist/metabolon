Transforming Biological Discovery: How has multiomic integration changed the way we explore biology? The scientific community has invested billions of dollars in understanding the blueprints of biology through genetics, and that work has generated extraordinary insight. At the same time, there is still much we do not fully understand, from gene function to which variants are benign and which are truly pathogenic.
